What does it add?

Stained-glass windows! Create window frames and fill each section with glass of different colours.

There are 6 different shapes a frame can have, each adding their own feeling. There's a lot of room for creativity, even in small spaces. Or, you can "paint" entire scenes in a larger space.

Why make this?

I wanted to make something for a yearly creative event in a group I'm in, and thought a Minecraft mod would be a fun thing to do. I love glass as an artform, so thought it might be fun to make more customisable stained glass windows.

This is the second mod I've made that adds something to the game, and has helped me learn more about making mods. I will continue making smaller projects like this to get more confident before I start on something much larger. I don't know the exact shape of that larger thing yet.
